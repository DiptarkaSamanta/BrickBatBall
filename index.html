📖 INSTRUCTIONS: BRICKS OF EGYPT & JARDINAINS

🏺 Bricks of Egypt Mechanics

• Sandstone Hieroglyphs: Destroy Ancient Egyptian blocks engraved with Ankhs, Eyes of Horus, Scarabs, Sun Discs, and Falcons!

• 🔥 Fire Bricks & 💧 Water Drops: Fire bricks are flaming blocks! When a Water Drop item falls and you catch it with your paddle, a cool mist sweeps across the board, extinguishing all fire bricks!

• Falling Treasures & Scarabs: When bricks shatter, golden scarabs, silver scarabs, rubies, emeralds, and diamonds cascade down. Catch them for massive score bonuses (+500 to +2,500 pts)!

• 𓂀 Guardian Eye of Horus: Collect the Eye relic to summon a floating guardian eye that bounces around the upper tomb, firing solar beams to destroy bricks!

• 8 Classic Level Packs: Play through Tutankhamen, Temple Mayhem, Echo Chambers, Fire on the Rocks, Pyramid of Madness, Nefertiti, Pharaoh's Curse, and The Eye of Horus!

🧙‍♂️ Jardinains! Mechanics

• The Gnomes ('Nains): Gnomes live inside and atop the bricks, heckling you and throwing flowerpots at your paddle. If a pot hits you, your paddle is stunned for 1.6s! Dodge them!

• Gnome Juggling: Knock gnomes off bricks with your ball, then catch and bounce them with your paddle. Each consecutive juggle multiplies your points: 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600... Bouncing them 3+ times drops guaranteed power-ups, and 5+ juggles ascends them into orbit for +5,000 points!
