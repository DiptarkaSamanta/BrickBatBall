📖 HOW TO PLAY: CHRONO BREAKER

🎮 CONTROLS

• Mouse / Arrow Keys / A & D: Move the paddle horizontally across the dimensional field.

• Left Click / Spacebar: Launch active or stuck ball, fire laser cannons.

• P / Esc: Pause & resume game.

• R: Restart current level.

🧱 SPECIAL BRICK MECHANICS

• Stone Bricks: Multi-hit armored blocks requiring 2-3 strikes. Fissures appear progressively.

• Iron Bricks: Indestructible to normal balls. Shattered by explosions or laser fire.

• Explosive TNT: Detonates in a wide fiery shockwave, triggering chain explosions!

• Moving Bricks: Patrol back and forth horizontally across the row.

• Invisible Bricks: Cloaked in temporal stealth; reveals faintly when ball strikes nearby.

• Regenerating Bricks: Self-repairs 6.5s after being broken unless level is cleared!

• Portal Bricks: Paired wormholes that teleport the ball across the screen with velocity preserved!

• Crystal Bricks: Shatter to release collectible 💎 Chrono Crystals used for permanent upgrades!

⚡ POWER-UPS

• Multi-Ball (⚡3): Spawns 2 extra balls at diverging angles.

• Fireball (🔥): Slices through destructible bricks without deflecting.

• Wide Paddle (⬌): Expands paddle width by 50%.

• Magnet (🧲): Catches ball upon impact; aim and press Space/Click to relaunch.

• Laser Cannons (⚡): Mounts twin blaster cannons on paddle wings.

• Slow Motion (⏳): Dilates time, reducing ball speed by 50% for high precision.

• Time Freeze (❄️): Freezes all moving hazards, obstacles and boss attacks.

• Energy Barrier (🛡️): Energy net at bottom of screen that bounces falling balls back up.

💀 WORLD BOSSES

Every 10th level features a supreme Dimension Boss (Sphinx, Goliath, Wyrm, Core, Devourer, Architect) with dynamic health bars, attack patterns, and projectile barrages to dodge!
