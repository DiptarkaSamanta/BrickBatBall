# BRICKS OF EGYPT & JARDINAINS! - Classic Arcade Suite

A dual-arcade recreation containing the **full authentic Bricks of Egypt game** (Arcade Lab, 2004) alongside the **full Jardinains! game** (Magic Chopstick Games, 2001), plus a combined **Mashup Mode**!

Switch between games instantly anytime using the top navigation bar or hotkeys **1**, **2**, and **3**!

## 🏺 GAME 1: BRICKS OF EGYPT (Authentic 2004 Game)

Play the full, authentic **Bricks of Egypt** brick breaker without gnomes, focusing on ancient Egyptian brick destruction, falling treasures, fire bricks, and relics!

### Features & Mechanics:
* **8 Classic Level Packs (40 Handcrafted Levels):**
  1. *Tutankhamen* (Levels 1–5)
  2. *Temple Mayhem* (Levels 6–10)
  3. *Echo Chambers* (Levels 11–15)
  4. *Fire on the Rocks* (Levels 16–20)
  5. *Pyramid of Madness* (Levels 21–25)
  6. *Nefertiti's Vault* (Levels 26–30)
  7. *Pharaoh's Curse* (Levels 31–35)
  8. *The Eye of Horus* (Levels 36–40)
* **🔥 Fire Bricks & 💧 Water Drops:**
  * Fire Bricks burn with active animated flames.
  * Destroying bricks can release a **Water Drop token [💧]**. Catching it with your bat instantly extinguishes all fire bricks across the board with a soothing steam hiss, transforming them into breakable sandstone!
* **💎 Falling Treasures & Scarabs:**
  * Shattering Egyptian blocks unleashes cascading ancient treasures. Catch them with your bat for major score multipliers:
    * 🪲 **Gold Scarabs:** +1,000 points
    * 🪲 **Silver Scarabs:** +500 points
    * 💎 **Diamonds:** +2,500 points
    * 🔴 **Rubies:** +1,500 points
    * 🟢 **Emeralds:** +1,000 points
    * 🔵 **Sapphires:** +800 points
* **𓂀 The Guardian Eye of Horus:**
  * Collect the Eye relic to summon a floating, autonomous Guardian Eye that glides across the tomb, periodically zapping bricks with solar beams!
* **⚡ Egyptian Harpoon Lasers & Scepter Bat:**
  * Equip your winged Egyptian stone bat with twin golden harpoon cannons to blast rows of bricks directly.
* **🔥 Fire of Ra (Fireball):**
  * Blazing solar fireball that plows through rows of sandstone, cartouches, and sarcophagi without deflecting.
* **☥ Ankh of Immortality:** Extra life tokens.

## 🧙‍♂️ GAME 2: JARDINAINS! (Classic 2001 Gnome Brick Breaker)

Switch to **Jardinains!** at any moment (press **2** or click the top tab) to play the classic garden brick breaker!

### Features & Mechanics:
* **The Gnomes ('Nains):** Gnomes live on the bricks, laughing, sticking their tongues out, and throwing terracotta flowerpots down at your paddle!
* **Flowerpot Hazards:** Dodge falling pots—if one strikes your paddle, you are stunned for 1.6 seconds and lose 250 points!
* **Gnome Juggling:** Knock gnomes off bricks and bounce them repeatedly on your paddle for escalating scores: **100, 200, 400, 800, 1,600, 3,200...** Bouncing a gnome 3+ times drops a guaranteed powerup; 5+ juggles ascends them into orbit for +5,000 points!
* **30 Handcrafted Garden Levels:** *First Garden*, *Gnome Outpost*, *Flower Power*, *Smiley Day*, *Pyramid of Nains*, *Castle Moat*, *Space Invader Nain*, *King Nain's Throne*, and more!
* **Procedural Gnome Voices & Garden Chiptune Music:** Authentic screams, boing spring bounces, chuckles, and retro tunes.

## ⚡ GAME 3: MASHUP (Gnomes in Egypt)

Press **3** or click **Mashup** to combine both worlds:
* Mischievous gnomes invade the Pharaoh's tombs!
* Gnomes sit on hieroglyphic sandstone blocks and fire bricks, throwing canopic jars and scarabs.
* Juggle **Mummy Gnomes** (unraveling linen bandage strips!) and **Pharaoh Gnomes** while collecting falling jewels, water drops, and summoning the Guardian Eye!

## 🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

| Key / Action | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Switch to **Bricks of Egypt** |
| **2** | Switch to **Jardinains!** |
| **3** | Switch to **Mashup Mode** |
| **Mouse / Arrow Keys / A & D** | Move paddle / bat |
| **Left Click / Spacebar** | Launch ball & fire lasers / harpoons |
| **P** | Pause / Resume |
| **M** | Toggle Mute |
| **L** | **Level Select** (Browse all 8 Egypt packs & 30 Garden levels) |
| **E** | **Level Editor** (Create & play custom levels) |
| **R** | Restart current level |
| **N** | Cheat skip / win level |
| **H** | Instructions & Power-up Guide |

## ✏️ Interactive Level Editor

Press **E** to open the built-in level editor:
* Paint with all Egyptian hieroglyph blocks (Ankh, Eye of Horus, Scarab, Sun of Ra, Cartouches, Sarcophagi, Urns, Fire Bricks) and Garden blocks.
* Place or remove all characters (Normal, Grumpy, Speedy, King, Mummy, Pharaoh, Anubis).
* Test play custom levels instantly, or export/import JSON level codes.
